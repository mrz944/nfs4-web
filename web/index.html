      how to race online

      1. Get the game into this browser. Install NFS: High Stakes (or Road Challenge) with the Modern Patch beta, click Choose NFS4 Folder… and pick that folder. It is copied into this browser once; next time it is already here.
      2. Pick a name. Type it under Online Play → Your name. It's what the others see in the room, and the game's own name dialog offers it too.
      3. Make a room. One player clicks Create Room, gives it a name, and optionally a password and a player limit (2–8). Untick Show in the room list for a private room.
      4. Bring your friends in. Click Copy invite link and send it, or let them pick the room in the Rooms list and click Join. Everyone in the room is listed with their ping and direct or via server.
      5. Everyone clicks Start Game. Wait until all players see the game's menu.
      6. Host the race. The player shown as Best host goes to Connect Players → Network (IPX) → Create Game, names the game, and in Host Game picks Positional Updates. For two players Peer-to-Peer, for more Client/Server.
      7. Join the race. The others go to Connect Players → Network (IPX), click Refresh Game List, pick the game under Games Available and click Join Game.
      8. Race. Everyone clicks Ready to Race in the multiplayer lobby, then the host picks the track and clicks Start Race. Single Race, Hot Pursuit (one of you can drive the cop car) and High Stakes all work online.

      Positional or Controller updates? NFS4 offers two netcodes. With Positional Updates every machine drives its own car and sends where it is, so your car reacts instantly whoever hosts. Controller Updates is the NFS3-style lockstep: the host's car reacts instantly and everyone else waits one round trip to the host. Over the internet, Positional is the better choice.

      original game · our work

      untouched: the real nfs4

      • Handling, physics and damage: every car drives exactly like on a 1999 PC. The original code runs, recompiled, not rewritten or re-tuned.
      • Cars, tracks, AI and police: the original game data, opponents and Hot Pursuit, including driving the cop car.
      • Music and sound: the original soundtrack and effects through the game's own sound engine.
      • Menus and modes: Career, Single Race, Hot Pursuit, High Stakes, Knockout, Tournament and the car Showcase.

      added: what makes it run here

      • Static recompilation: the game's x86 code, MMX included, is translated to C and WebAssembly, so it runs natively in the browser with no PC emulator.
      • Voodoo2 on your GPU: the 3dfx Glide graphics path is redrawn with WebGL 2, keeping the Voodoo fog, depth and blending.
      • Single-core scheduling: NFS4 expects one CPU core, so its threads take turns just like on a 1999 PC. That fixes the streamer and deadlock bugs it has on modern machines.
      • Internet multiplayer: the game's LAN (IPX) mode now works over the internet. Rooms with passwords, a room browser, direct browser-to-browser links, lost packets recovered in one tick, and a best-host pick.

      Built on the NFS4 Modern Patch by VEG (based on 4.50: memory limit, hardware detection and start-up fixes). Your game folder needs this patch.

      read me

      This page runs Need for Speed: High Stakes (PC, version 4.50 with the modern patch; Road Challenge in Europe) straight in your browser: the original game code is recompiled to WebAssembly, and the Voodoo2 graphics go through WebGL. You bring your own copy: pick your game folder once and it is kept in this browser's private storage. Nothing is uploaded.
